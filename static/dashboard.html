Declared incidents

Events from both the social-engineering honeypot and technical feeds (auth logs, brute-force detectors) are correlated on shared indicators — a phishing domain seen in a scam conversation and in an auth-failure burst collapses into one Mixed incident. Each incident is scored 0–100 and given a prioritised response plan: what to do, to which target, by whom, by when.

    Multi-language detector

    Paste a scam message in any supported language — English, Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu or Marathi, native script or romanized.

    URL / Domain Scanner

    Paste any suspicious URL or domain. Analysis is entirely offline — no DNS lookups, no outbound HTTP calls, zero data leakage, air-gap safe. Signals: abused TLDs (.tk .xyz .ml), brand impersonation (SBI, HDFC, IRCTC…), suspicious keywords, IP-as-host, subdomain depth, hyphen density, non-standard ports. Threats feed into the incident correlator automatically.

    Email Phishing Analyser

    Paste a raw email (headers + body — use "View Source" or "Show Original" in your mail client). Checks display-name spoofing, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, reply-to mismatch and suspicious URLs.
