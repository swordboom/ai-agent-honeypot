Feature 3: Declare an Incident and Say How Serious It Is
Right now: The system detects individual scam messages. It never officially says "this is an attack" as a whole
event
What's missing: Once related alerts are grouped, someone needs to raise a flag and say: "This group of alerts =
one attack. And it is CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW severity."
What to add: An incident creator that:

. Groups the connected alerts into one named incident
. Scores how dangerous it is (based on how fast it's moving, what kind of attack, how many systems affected)
. Labels it clearly so operators know what to prioritise first


. Feature 11: Multi-Language Scam Detection
The idea: Most Indian scammers don't operate in English. They use Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi. Right
now your system only understands English scam messages.
This feature makes your system detect scams in all major Indian languages - because a scammer saying
"aapka account band ho jayega" is just as dangerous as "your account will be blocked."
Why judges love this: This is India-specific innovation. No generic global product does this well. You'd be
solving a real Indian problem.
